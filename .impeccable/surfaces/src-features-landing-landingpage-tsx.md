# Surface brief — Landing (/) and demo invitation (/demo)

Scope: commercial landing page (mode: Persuade) and the demo invitation (mode: Experience). Audience: couples in France choosing a digital wedding invitation; guests opening an invitation on iPhone. Action: open the demo, then order a plan (Essential €49 / Signature €99 / Couture €179). Proof: the working demo invitation itself; no testimonials exist and none may be invented. Constraints: brief-pinned palette and world (see PRODUCT.md, Brand Commitments). Direction pinned by the brief, so concept-seed was not rolled. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The invitation is an object of light, not a page. A living glass-and-chrome sculpture is the product and the proof. This refuses the category default of a cream card with script type and a photo carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian ground (#0B0B12) owns every surface. Pearl type, with Iris used only as a light colour (sculpture glow, focus, active states) and Chrome for hairline metal (orbit ring, rules, outlined numerals). Bodoni Moda at monumental scale for names and statements, Jost for everything that works. Iridescence lives in the 3D material, never in text. Pill controls in pearl, film grain at the edge of perception.

STORY: In five seconds the visitor sees a sculpture reacting to their finger and the line "Your moment. Another dimension.", understands that VOW. makes immersive invitations, opens the demo, believes guests will be moved and that their privacy is protected, then picks a plan.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Landing: the sculpture fills the right two thirds (centred and higher on mobile). The two-line headline is set bottom-left at monumental size, the second line indented and italic. The primary action "Ouvrir l'invitation démo" is a pearl pill beside the subcopy; the secondary action leads to the plans. Demo: black screen, floating sculpture, particles, the opening line, and an "Open the invitation" control at the optical centre.

FORM: brief-pinned direction (Digital Art / Futuristic Couture); position 1 of 1. seed: none (direction pinned by brief; roll not run)

Signature interaction: the opening. The sculpture inflates with light, a pearl-iris flash crosses the screen, and the couple's names rise from depth (blur and scale settling to sharp). Motion grammar: expo-out from visible defaults; one authored moment per page; reduced motion swaps it for a crossfade.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
